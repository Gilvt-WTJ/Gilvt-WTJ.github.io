FAQ

Where is my progress saved? Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Does the game have ads? Only optional reward videos: you choose to watch one for a bonus (15 more seconds, double fragments, or an extra revive). The game never shows an ad on its own, and you can always play without watching any.

Are there in-app purchases? No.

Does it work offline? Yes. Without a connection the game plays normally; only the optional reward videos are unavailable.

How do I change the language? Use the language button in the settings / pause menu (English, 中文).

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent the first time it starts. To change your choice later, delete and reinstall the app, or contact us.

常见问题

进度存在哪里？只存在你的设备上。没有账号，也没有云存档，删除 App 会同时删除进度。

有广告吗？只有可选的奖励视频：你自己选择看，看完得到奖励（多 15 秒、碎片翻倍、多一次复活）。游戏不会自己弹广告，不看也能完整地玩。

有内购吗？没有。

能离线玩吗？可以。没有网络时游戏照常玩，只是看不了奖励视频。

怎么切换语言？在设置 / 暂停菜单里点语言按钮（English、中文）。

怎么修改广告隐私选择？在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，第一次打开 App 时会询问你是否同意。之后想修改，可以删除并重新安装 App，或者联系我们。
