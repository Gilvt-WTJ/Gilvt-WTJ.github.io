Privacy Policy

Otter Orbit ("the app") does not ask for an account and we, the developer, do not collect any personal data. The app shows optional reward videos through Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What the app stores on your device

Your game progress and settings (levels, stars, town, heroes, language, sound on/off) are saved only on your device, using the standard iOS app storage. This data is never sent to us and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Reward videos are optional: an ad is shown only when you tap a button to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect:

  • device information and identifiers (such as the app-scoped identifier for vendors; the app does not ask for tracking permission, so the advertising identifier is not available to it),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions (for example, that an ad was shown or completed),
  • diagnostic and performance data.

The app requests non-personalized ads only and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google Privacy Policy.

What the app does not do

No account or login. No in-app purchases. No access to your contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone. The app itself does not send any data to us.

Children

The app is a general-audience game. Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released, and the date above will change.

隐私政策

水獭拾星（Otter Orbit，以下简称「本 App」）不需要账号，我们（开发者）不收集任何个人数据。本 App 通过 Google AdMob 提供可选的奖励视频，AdMob 会按下面的说明收集一些数据。

本 App 在你的设备上保存什么

游戏进度和设置（关卡、星星、小镇、角色、语言、音效开关）只用 iOS 标准的 App 存储保存在你的设备上，不会发送给我们，删除 App 时会一起删除。

广告（Google AdMob）

奖励视频是可选的：只有你点按钮选择观看时才会播放。广告由 Google AdMob 提供。为了投放和统计广告、防止作弊，Google 的 SDK 可能会收集：

  • 设备信息和标识符（例如 App 范围内的供应商标识符；本 App 不请求追踪权限，因此拿不到广告标识符），
  • 根据 IP 地址推断的大概位置，
  • 广告互动（例如广告是否展示、是否看完），
  • 诊断和性能数据。

本 App 只请求非个性化广告，不会跨其他公司的 App 或网站追踪你。在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士，请求广告之前会通过 Google 的同意表单征求你的同意。Google 如何使用数据：policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites、Google 隐私权政策。

本 App 不做什么

没有账号和登录；没有内购；不访问通讯录、照片、精确位置、相机或麦克风；本 App 自己不向我们发送任何数据。

儿童

本 App 是面向所有人的游戏。广告只投放适合大众的内容，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。
